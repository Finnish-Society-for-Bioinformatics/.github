The Finnish Society for Bioinformatics was founded in 2006 with the aim of
bringing together everyone interested in bioinformatics. For almost two
decades, our mission has remained the same: to support research, share the
latest scientific discoveries, and provide opportunities for exchanging ideas
and knowledge.

We organize regular webinars, seminars, and training sessions, share job
opportunities for our members, and host networking and collaboration events.
Every two years, we proudly recognize outstanding work through our Best
Bioinformatics Thesis Award.

Our monthly BioBeer gatherings in Helsinki, Turku, Tampere, and Oulu offer a
relaxed way to meet and connect with fellow bioinformaticians.

Whether you're an experienced researcher, an early-career student, a research
group, or a company, we warmly welcome you to join our community and be part of
this bioinformatics network.
